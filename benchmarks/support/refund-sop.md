# Northwind Software Refund Policy

POL-REF-3, version 2.1. This policy governs product returns and refunds for all Northwind plans.

## 1. Return window

Standard-plan customers may request a return within 30 days of the purchase date.
Pro-plan customers may request a return within 60 days of the purchase date.
The return window is counted in calendar days from the date of purchase.

## 2. Item condition

Refunds require the product in its original sealed packaging.
An item whose seal is broken or that has been opened is final sale and is not refundable.

## 3. Outside this policy

Gift returns are issued as store credit only, in all cases.
Price adjustments are honored within 14 days of purchase, separately from returns.
Licenses purchased through resellers follow the reseller's own refund terms.
