# Acme Cloud Support Service Level Agreement

SOP-SLA-7, version 1.2. This document governs first-response commitments and service
credits for all Acme Cloud support plans.

## 1. First-response clocks

Enterprise customers with P1 issues receive a first response within 4 business hours.
Enterprise customers with P2 issues receive a first response within 8 business hours.
Team customers with P1 issues receive a first response within 2 business hours.
Team customers with P2 issues receive a first response within 6 business hours.
Starter customers with P1 or P2 issues receive a first response within 24 business hours.

## 2. Business hours and holidays

Business hours for the default calendar are 09:00 to 18:00 on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday.
All times on the default calendar are UTC.
The default calendar observes 2026-12-25 as a holiday.
The default calendar observes 2026-01-01 as a holiday.
Customers on the follow-the-sun calendar are covered by their contract appendix, not this section.

## 3. Service credits

A breach of 60 or more business minutes earns a credit of 10 percent of the monthly fee.
A breach of 240 or more business minutes earns a credit of 25 percent of the monthly fee.
A breach of 480 or more business minutes earns a credit of 50 percent of the monthly fee.
Credits are issued on the next invoice after the breach review closes.
