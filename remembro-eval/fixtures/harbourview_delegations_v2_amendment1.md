# Harbourview Community Foundation

## Amendment No. 1 to the Financial Delegations Schedule (HCF-FIN-02, version 2.0)

**Effective date:** 1 November 2026
**Approved by:** Board of Trustees, resolution 2026/19

### 1. Amendments

The Chief Operating Officer is authorised to approve operational expenditure up to NZD
80,000 per transaction. This replaces the limit stated in clause 3.2 of the Schedule.

The Grants Manager is authorised to approve grant expenditure up to NZD 20,000 per grant.
This replaces the limit stated in clause 3.3 of the Schedule.

### 2. Office holders

Julian Ford ceased to hold the role of Facilities Coordinator on 31 October 2026. Marcus
Bell was appointed Facilities Coordinator with effect from 1 November 2026.

### 3. Suspension lifted

The suspension of Owen Pritchard's approval authority under clause 6.1 of the Schedule,
which took effect on 10 August 2026, was lifted with effect from 1 September 2026.

### 4. Interpretation

Except as amended above, the Schedule continues in force. Where this Amendment and the
Schedule differ, this Amendment prevails from its effective date.
