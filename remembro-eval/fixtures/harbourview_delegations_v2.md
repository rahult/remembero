# Harbourview Community Foundation

## Financial Delegations Schedule

**Reference:** HCF-FIN-02
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective date:** 1 March 2026
**Approved by:** Board of Trustees, resolution 2026/04
**Owner:** Chief Operating Officer

### 1. Scope

This Schedule sets out who may approve expenditure on behalf of the Foundation, the
limits that apply, and the conditions under which those limits may be varied. It applies to
all staff, trustees and contractors. Amounts are stated in New Zealand dollars (NZD) and
are inclusive of GST unless stated otherwise. Where this Schedule is silent, the matter must
be referred to the Board of Trustees.

### 3. Standing delegations

#### 3.1 Chief Executive Officer

The Chief Executive Officer is authorised to approve operational expenditure up to NZD
150,000 per transaction. The Chief Executive Officer is authorised to approve capital
expenditure up to NZD 300,000 per transaction. The Chief Executive Officer is authorised to
approve grant expenditure up to NZD 200,000 per grant.

#### 3.2 Chief Operating Officer

The Chief Operating Officer is authorised to approve operational expenditure up to NZD
60,000 per transaction. The Chief Operating Officer is authorised to approve capital
expenditure up to NZD 40,000 per transaction. The Chief Operating Officer shall not approve
grant expenditure.

#### 3.3 Grants Manager

The Grants Manager is authorised to approve grant expenditure up to NZD 25,000 per grant
provided the grant falls within a program approved by the Board. The Grants Manager is
authorised to approve operational expenditure up to NZD 10,000 per transaction. The Grants
Manager shall not approve capital expenditure.

#### 3.4 Facilities Coordinator

The Facilities Coordinator is authorised to approve facilities maintenance classified as
operational expenditure up to NZD 5,000 per transaction.

#### 3.5 Above the limits

Expenditure above the limits in this section requires the approval of the Board of Trustees.

### 4. Conditions applying to every delegation

No delegate shall approve a payment to themselves, to a relative, or to an entity in which
they hold an interest. A transaction shall not be split to bring it within a limit. Grant
expenditure above NZD 100,000 requires two signatures. An approval given outside these
conditions is void.

### 6. Suspension and revocation

#### 6.1 Suspension

The approval authority of Owen Pritchard is suspended with effect from 10 August 2026
pending the outcome of an internal review, until lifted in writing by the Board of
Trustees. A person whose authority is suspended shall not exercise authority held directly
or by delegation for the duration of the suspension.

#### 6.2 Revocation generally

A person who has delegated or authorised authority under this Schedule may revoke it at
any time by written notice to the Chief Operating Officer. Revocation takes effect from the
date stated in the notice.

Kessler under clause 5.2 with effect from 1 August 2026. From that date the Grants

### 7. Records and interpretation

All approvals are recorded in the finance system within five business days. The Chief
Operating Officer reports exceptions to the Board of Trustees each quarter. For the
avoidance of doubt, the Chief Operating Officer's limit for capital expenditure is NZD
50,000 per transaction. Where a summary table in an appendix differs from the body of this
Schedule, the body prevails.
