# Northwind Holdings Pty Ltd

## Financial Delegation of Authority Policy

**Document reference:** FIN-POL-007
**Version:** 1.4
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Owner:** Office of the Chief Financial Officer
**Review cycle:** Annual

### 1. Purpose

This policy sets out who within Northwind Holdings Pty Ltd ("Northwind", "the Company") may
commit the Company to expenditure, the limits that apply to each level of authority, and the
circumstances in which authority may be delegated, suspended or withdrawn. It applies to all
employees, contractors and officers of the Company and its wholly owned subsidiaries.

Nothing in this policy creates an entitlement to any role, and the Board retains the right to
vary any delegation at any time by resolution.

### 2. Scope and definitions

For the purposes of this policy:

- "Operational expenditure" means recurring expenditure incurred in the ordinary course of
  business, including salaries, rent, utilities, subscriptions, travel and consumables.
- "Capital expenditure" means expenditure on assets with a useful life exceeding twelve
  months, including plant, equipment, vehicles, software licences of more than one year and
  leasehold improvements.
- "Approve" means to authorise a commitment of Company funds by signing a purchase order,
  contract, invoice or expense claim, whether physically or through the Company's finance
  system.
- "Delegate" means to grant another person the temporary right to exercise authority that
  the delegating person holds under this policy.

All amounts in this policy are expressed in Australian dollars (AUD) and are exclusive of
GST unless otherwise stated.

### 4. Standing approval authorities

#### 4.1 Operational expenditure

The Chief Financial Officer may approve operational expenditure up to AUD 100,000 per
transaction.

The Finance Director may approve operational expenditure up to AUD 50,000 per transaction.

The Procurement Manager may approve operational expenditure up to AUD 20,000 per transaction,
provided the expenditure relates to goods or services procured under an approved supplier
agreement.

Operational expenditure above AUD 100,000 requires approval by the Board.

#### 4.2 Capital expenditure

Capital expenditure is treated separately from operational expenditure and the operational
limits in section 4.1 do not apply to it.

The Chief Financial Officer may approve capital expenditure up to AUD 250,000 per project.

The Finance Director may approve capital expenditure up to AUD 75,000 per project.

The Procurement Manager may not approve capital expenditure of any amount.

Capital expenditure above AUD 250,000 requires approval by the Board.

#### 4.3 Prohibitions applying to all approvers

No person may approve expenditure from which they, a member of their immediate family, or an
entity in which they hold a material interest would benefit. Such expenditure must be
referred to the next higher level of authority.

Procurement Managers must not approve expenditure benefiting themselves under any
circumstances, and must not split a purchase into smaller transactions in order to bring it
within their limit.

An approver must not approve their own expense claims.

### 5. Delegation of authority

#### 5.1 General conditions

A role holder may delegate all or part of their approval authority to another employee for a
defined period not exceeding thirty days, by written notice to the Financial Controller
stating the delegate, the scope of authority delegated, and the start and end dates.

A delegate may not further delegate authority they hold by delegation.

Delegated authority is exercised subject to every limit and prohibition that applies to the
delegating role holder, and to any narrower limit stated in the notice of delegation.

#### 5.2 Delegations in force at the date of this version

During Alice Morgan's period of leave from 1 September 2026 to 20 September 2026, Alice
delegates to Bob Chen her authority to approve operational expenditure. Under this
delegation Bob Chen may approve operational expenditure up to AUD 100,000 per transaction.

The delegation in the preceding paragraph does not extend to capital expenditure. Bob Chen's
authority for capital expenditure during that period remains the Finance Director limit in
section 4.2.

Carol Evans may approve invoices for operational expenditure up to AUD 30,000 per transaction
between 1 September 2026 and 15 September 2026, while the Accounts Payable function is
understaffed. This is a temporary extension of the Procurement Manager limit and lapses
automatically on 15 September 2026.

D. Smith may approve utilities invoices classified as operational expenditure up to AUD 5,000
per transaction from 1 September 2026 to 30 September 2026 while the Accounts Payable backlog
is cleared.

#### 5.3 Notification

The Financial Controller shall record every delegation in the delegation register and notify
the Accounts Payable Lead. The Accounts Payable Lead is responsible for ensuring that the
finance system reflects delegations in force. The Accounts Payable Lead does not hold
approval authority under this policy.

### 6. Suspension and revocation

#### 6.1 Suspension

The Board or the Chief Executive Officer may suspend a person's approval authority pending
the outcome of an investigation. A suspended person retains their role but may not exercise
any approval authority, whether held directly or by delegation, for the duration of the
suspension.

Carol Evans's approval authority is suspended with effect from 8 September 2026 until further
notice pending review of supplier onboarding procedures. Carol Evans remains the Procurement
Manager during the suspension.

#### 6.2 Revocation

A delegating role holder may revoke a delegation at any time by written notice to the
Financial Controller. Revocation takes effect from the date stated in the notice or, if no
date is stated, from the date the notice is received.

#### 6.3 Amendment to the delegation in section 5.2

By notice dated 10 September 2026, Alice Morgan revoked the delegation of operational
expenditure authority to Bob Chen described in section 5.2, with effect from 12 September
2026. From that date Bob Chen's authority for operational expenditure reverts to the Finance
Director limit in section 4.1. This amendment supersedes the end date stated in section 5.2.

### 7. Records and review

All approvals must be recorded in the finance system within two business days. The Financial
Controller will sample approvals quarterly and report exceptions to the Audit and Risk
Committee. Capital expenditure approved by the Finance Director is capped at AUD 100,000 per
project and must be reported to the Committee at its next meeting. This policy will be reviewed annually or earlier if the Company's structure
changes materially.

Questions about this policy should be directed to the Office of the CFO. Requests to vary a
limit must be made in writing and approved by the Board. The Company's general travel policy,
gifts and hospitality policy and procurement policy continue to apply alongside this
document. Employees are reminded that the Company's values of integrity, accountability and
respect apply to every financial decision.
