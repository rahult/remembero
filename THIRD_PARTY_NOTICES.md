# Third-party evaluation assets

Remembero's source code is MIT licensed. The selected document images in the local document
intelligence showcase retain their own source terms and are not relicensed under MIT.

## IRS Form W-9

- English source: <https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw9.pdf>
- Spanish source: <https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw9sp.pdf>
- Publisher: United States Internal Revenue Service

The files are official United States government forms. Source attribution is preserved. No
endorsement by the IRS is implied, and IRS names or marks remain subject to applicable rules.

## MathBridge

- Paper: *MathBridge: A Large Corpus Dataset for Translating Spoken Mathematical Expressions
  into LaTeX Formulas for Improved Readability*
- Authors: Kyudan Jung, Sieun Hyeon, Jeong Youn Kwon, Nam-Joon Kim, Hyun Gon Ryu, Hyuk-Jae Lee,
  and Jaeyoung Do
- Source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07081>
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The packaged page image is a 150-DPI rendering of PDF page 4 and is attributed here. It is used
as an evaluation example; Remembero has added interactive evidence overlays but has not altered
the underlying page content.

## United Nations multilingualism publication

- Publisher: United Nations Dag Hammarskjöld Library
- Source: <https://www.un.org/sites/un2.un.org/files/2227864e.pdf>
- Selected source: PDF page 20, printed page 15

The publication states that it may be reused for non-commercial purposes with credit to the
United Nations Dag Hammarskjöld Library. The packaged page image is therefore an attributed,
non-commercial evaluation asset and is not covered by Remembero's MIT license. Commercial
redistributors should remove or replace this image unless they have confirmed separate rights.

## Distribution boundary

The npm package excludes every original third-party PDF and duplicate benchmark-page raster.
Only the four selected web-console page images are packaged. Source URLs, SHA-256 values, page
numbers, and rights notes remain available in the product and provenance manifest.
